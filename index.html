About the studio

A collaborative studio.

AKS & Co is founded and run by Ayhan Konar, a designer and developer with 7 years of experience in engineering and over 15 years in design and illustration. The studio publishes software for iPhone and iPad through the App Store.

The "& Co" is the collaborators, testers and friends who shape every release before it ships. It is also you. If you have an idea worth building, or a project that needs design and engineering under one roof, let's work together.
